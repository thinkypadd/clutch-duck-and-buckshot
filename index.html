ONE HAND. FOUR JOBS.

Survive 3 laps against 5 rival cars using only your mouse hand. Every action leaves the other stations unattended.

FOOTWELL PEDALS (BOTTOM LEFT)
Hold GAS to build speed. Let go and coast while your hand moves elsewhere. Hold BRK for sharp turns.
STEERING WHEEL (LEFT)
Grab and rotate to corner. When released, the wheel slowly self-centers while road bumps tug your line.
NITRO T-HANDLE (RIGHT)
Drag all the way down to trigger boost. Pull repeatedly to stack up to 3X speed with heavy cabin shake.
VISOR SHOTGUN (TOP RIGHT)
Click holster to equip, Left-Click road to fire. When empty, click holster to dock open, drag 2 spent hulls out, drag 2 fresh shells from strap into breech, and click gun to snap shut.

CABIN OPERATING MANUAL

  • Drive vs. Shoot: While holding the sawed-off shotgun, your hand cannot touch the wheel, pedals, or nitro.
  • Reloading Under Fire: Once docked open on the sun visor, your hand is free between shell drags to correct steering or tap gas.
  • Shotgun Range: Point-blank blasts wreck rivals in 1 shot. Mid-range blasts smoke them first, then wreck on the 2nd hit.
  • Nitro Risk: Nitro pressure regenerates automatically. 3X stack delivers maximum top speed and violent road drift.
  • Shortcuts: Press Esc to pause or close dialogs, M to toggle sound.
